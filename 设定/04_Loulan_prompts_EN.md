## P1 Dead City, First Sight

Cinematic photorealistic concept art, 2.35:1 widescreen, muted desaturated palette, fine airborne dust, subtle film grain. Setting: a modern-day (year 2006) Chinese expedition team inside 2000-year-old Han-dynasty Loulan ruins, Western Regions desert, China. Lighting doctrine: warm amber kerosene-lantern light versus an eerie cold blue-green 'death-qi' ambient glow. Grounded archaeological realism. At the far end of a blood-red sunset horizon, the colossal silhouette of a ruined ancient city rises without warning from the desert. Broken walls half-buried in drifting sand; weathered giant timbers, eroded to bare skeletons, lean at angles like the ribs of a dead titan. In the foreground, a tiny silhouette of four travelers and one camel gives monumental scale. Wind-driven sand veils. Do NOT include: cartoon or anime style, glowing magic runes or circles, Western fantasy armor, any text or watermarks, extra limbs or fingers.
